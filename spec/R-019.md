# R-019 · Reinstate a policy cancelled for nonpayment, with or without a gap

## Rule

A cancelled policy can be put back in force (reinstated) only when all of these are true: it was
cancelled for nonpayment; the full amount owed has been paid; the payment came within 30 days after
the cancellation date; and the term has not ended. Otherwise the policyholder must apply for a new
policy.

- **Without a gap.** If the payment came within 10 days after the cancellation date and the
  policyholder signs a statement of no loss for the days since cancellation, coverage is restored
  from the cancellation date, as if the policy had never been cancelled.
- **With a gap.** Otherwise, coverage starts again at 12:01 a.m. on the day the payment is received.
  There is no coverage between the cancellation date and that day.

"Days after cancellation" is the day the payment is received minus the cancellation date. Only a
supervisor can reinstate without a gap (R-022).
