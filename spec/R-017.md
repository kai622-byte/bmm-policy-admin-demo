# R-017 · A cancellation notice says why, when and how to stop it

## Rule

Every cancellation notice from the company states the policy number, the policyholder's name and
address, the reason, and the date and time the cancellation takes effect (12:01 a.m.). A notice for
nonpayment also states the amount due and that paying it in full before the cancellation date keeps
the policy in force (R-018).

The system records the date the notice was mailed and keeps proof of mailing. While a notice is
outstanding, the policy shows as "Cancellation pending".
