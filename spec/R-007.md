# R-007 · Backdated changes need a supervisor's approval

## Rule

Send a mid-term change to a supervisor for approval when its effective date is more than 30 days
before the day it is requested. "Days backdated" is the day of the request minus the effective date.
The rule is the same for every kind of change.

## Calculation examples

| ID  | Change         | Days backdated | → Decision        | → Reason                    | Rationale                                  |
| --- | -------------- | -------------- | ----------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| E1  | Remove driver  | 45             | Send for approval | Backdated more than 30 days | Past the 30-day limit                      |
| E2  | Add vehicle    | 30             | Process           |                             | Exactly 30 days is not more than 30        |
| E3  | Add vehicle    | 31             | Send for approval | Backdated more than 30 days | One day past the limit                     |
| E4  | Change address | 0              | Process           |                             | Not backdated                              |
| E5  | Remove vehicle | -10            | Process           |                             | Dated 10 days in the future: not backdated |
