# R-020 · After a gap, the policyholder does not pay for the days without coverage

## Rule

When a policy is reinstated with a gap (R-019), the policyholder gets a premium credit for the days
without coverage: the full-term premium in proportion to those days. The days run from the
cancellation date up to, but not including, the day coverage starts again. The expiration date does
not change. The notice of reinstatement states the dates without coverage.

A policy reinstated without a gap gets no credit.

## Calculation examples

| --- | ----------------- | --------------------- | ------------ | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| E1  | $2,220.00         | 11                    | 182          | $134.18          | Cancelled 2027-02-25, coverage again from 2027-03-08 |
| E2  | $2,220.00         | 15                    | 182          | $182.97          |                                                      |
| E3  | $2,220.00         | 30                    | 182          | $365.93          | The longest gap allowed                              |
| E4  | $2,220.00         | 0                     | 182          | $0.00            | Reinstated without a gap                             |
