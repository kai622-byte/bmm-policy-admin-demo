# R-021 · Every transaction is kept in the policy's history

## Rule

The policy keeps a history of every transaction: new business, each change (issued or declined),
each cancellation notice, cancellation, withdrawal and reinstatement. Each entry shows what kind of
transaction it was, its effective date, the date it was entered, who entered it, who approved or
declined it (if anyone), the reason and the premium change.

Nothing in the history can be edited or deleted. A mistake is put right by a new transaction.
