# R-001 · Find a policy and see it as it stood on any date

## Rule

A service representative can find a policy by its number or by the policyholder's last name, and
open it. The policy opens as it stands today. The representative can also choose any date in the
term and see the policy exactly as it stood on that date: its drivers, vehicles, address and premium.

## Workflow scenarios

### S1 · Find a policy and look at it

Data: data/policy-PA-1001.json

| Step | Who         | Does                                             | Should see                                                                                                                                       | Checks |
| ---- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------ |
| 1    | Service rep | Searches for policy number PA-1001               | One result: PA-1001, Jordan Avery, Tampa, FL, In force                                                                                           |        |
| 2    | Service rep | Opens the policy                                 | Term 2026-12-01 to 2027-06-01; drivers Jordan Avery and Riley Avery; vehicles 2021 Toyota Camry and 2018 Honda CR-V; full-term premium $2,220.00 |        |
| 3    | Service rep | Goes back and searches for the last name "Avery" | PA-1001 is in the results                                                                                                                        |        |
