# R-006 · Issuing a change creates a new version and keeps the old one

## Rule

When a change is issued, the policy gets a new version that takes effect on the change's effective
date. Earlier versions are kept and can still be viewed (R-001). Each version records its effective
date, the date it was entered and who entered it.
