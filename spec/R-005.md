# R-005 · Show what a change will cost before it is issued

## Rule

Before a change is issued, the representative sees a quote: the additional or return premium for the
rest of the term (R-004), the new full-term premium, whether the change needs approval or a
statement of no loss (R-007 to R-009), the documents it will produce (R-012) and how it will be
billed (R-013). A quote changes nothing on the policy. It can be saved and issued later, or abandoned.
