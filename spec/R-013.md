# R-013 · Spread a premium change over the installments still to come

## Rule

**Additional premium.** Divide it equally over the installments not yet due. If it does not divide
evenly, the leftover cents go to the earliest installments, one cent each. If no installments are
left, bill the whole amount now, due 15 days after the change is issued.

**Return premium.** Reduce the installments not yet due equally, in the same way. If that would take
an installment below zero, or no installments are left, refund the rest to the policyholder.
