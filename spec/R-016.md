# R-016 · Notice before the company cancels for another reason depends on the state

## Rule

The company may cancel a policy mid-term for a reason other than nonpayment only when the reason is
on the permitted list: material misrepresentation or fraud, or a driver's license suspended or
revoked. The cancellation date can be no earlier than the mailing date plus the days of notice for
the policy's state:

| State      | Days of notice for other reasons |
| ---------- | -------------------------------- |
| California | 20                               |
| Florida    | 45                               |
| New York   | 20                               |
| Texas      | 10                               |

The days of notice and the permitted reasons are settings by state, as in R-015. The unused premium
is refunded as in R-014.
