# R-011 · A change dated before an earlier change re-prices that change

## Rule

Sometimes a change is requested with an effective date before a change that has already been issued.
This is called an out-of-sequence change. The system warns the representative and names the later
change. It then:

1. prices the new change from its own effective date, as usual (R-004);
2. prices the later change again, as if the new change had already been in place, and charges or
   returns the difference for the days left from the later change's effective date.

The quote shows both amounts and their total. All terms in the examples have 182 days.
