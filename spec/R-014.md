# R-014 · Cancel a policy at the policyholder's request

## Rule

A policyholder can ask to cancel on any date from the day of the request to the end of the term.
The cancellation takes effect at 12:01 a.m. on that date.

The unused premium is the full-term premium in proportion to the days left (R-003, R-004), with no
fee or penalty. The premium used is the full-term premium minus the unused premium. If the
policyholder has paid more than the premium used, refund the difference. If they have paid less,
they still owe the difference.

All terms in the examples have 182 days.
