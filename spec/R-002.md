# R-002 · A mid-term change takes effect on a date inside the term

## Rule

Every mid-term change has an effective date. The date must be on or after the first day of the term
and before the expiration date, and the policy must be in force on that date. A date in the future
is allowed. The change takes effect at 12:01 a.m. on its effective date.

The sample term runs from 2026-12-01 to 2027-06-01.

## Calculation examples

| ID  | Effective date | Policy status on that date | → Decision  | → Reason                                | Rationale                                          |
| --- | -------------- | -------------------------- | ----------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| E1  | 2027-03-03     | In force                   | Allowed     |                                         | Inside the term                                    |
| E2  | 2026-12-01     | In force                   | Allowed     |                                         | The first day of the term counts                   |
| E3  | 2026-11-30     | In force                   | Not allowed | Before the term starts                  |                                                    |
| E4  | 2027-06-01     | In force                   | Not allowed | On or after the expiration date         | Coverage ends at 12:01 a.m. on the expiration date |
| E5  | 2027-05-31     | In force                   | Allowed     |                                         | The last full day of the term                      |
| E6  | 2027-03-03     | Cancelled                  | Not allowed | The policy is not in force on that date |                                                    |
