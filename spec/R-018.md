# R-018 · Paying in full before the cancellation date stops the cancellation

## Rule

A cancellation for nonpayment is withdrawn when the full amount due is received before the
cancellation date. The policy then continues with no break in coverage. A smaller payment does not
stop the cancellation; it is applied to the amount owed. A payment received on or after the
cancellation date does not stop it either; the policy is cancelled, and reinstatement is a separate
decision (R-019).

| E4  | $370.00    | $400.00     | 2027-02-12       | 2027-02-25        | Cancellation withdrawn |                                        | An overpayment still counts as paid in full |
