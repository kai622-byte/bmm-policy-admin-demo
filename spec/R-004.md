# R-004 · Charge or return premium in proportion to the days left

## Rule

The premium for a mid-term change is the change in the full-term premium, multiplied by the days
left in the term (R-003) and divided by the days in the term. An increase is an additional premium;
a decrease is a return premium, shown as a negative amount. There is no fee or penalty.

The result is rounded to the cent. Exactly half a cent rounds away from zero.

## Calculation examples

| ID  | Change in full-term premium | Days left | Days in term | → Premium change | Rationale                                          |
| --- | --------------------------- | --------- | ------------ | ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| E1  | $1,060.00                   | 90        | 182          | $524.18          | Adding a vehicle                                   |
| E2  | -$980.00                    | 120       | 182          | -$646.15         | Removing a vehicle                                 |
| E3  | $45.50                      | 75        | 182          | $18.75           | A change of address                                |
| E4  | -$297.36                    | 130       | 182          | -$212.40         | Removing a driver                                  |
| E5  | $100.01                     | 91        | 182          | $50.01           | Exactly $50.005: half a cent rounds away from zero |
| E6  | -$100.01                    | 91        | 182          | -$50.01          | The same rounding for a return premium             |
| E7  | $1,060.00                   | 182       | 182          | $1,060.00        | A change on the first day costs the full amount    |
