# R-022 · Who may do what

## Rule

Service representatives handle everyday transactions. Supervisors can do everything a
representative can, and they alone approve or decline changes, start a company cancellation for a
reason other than nonpayment, and reinstate a policy without a gap. Nobody approves a change they
entered themselves (R-010).
