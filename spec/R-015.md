# R-015 · Notice before cancelling for nonpayment depends on the state

## Rule

When an installment is not paid by its due date, the company may cancel the policy, but only after
mailing the policyholder a notice. The cancellation date can be no earlier than the mailing date
plus the days of notice for the policy's state:

| State      | Days of notice for nonpayment |
| ---------- | ----------------------------- |
| California | 10                            |
| Florida    | 10                            |
| New York   | 15                            |
| Texas      | 10                            |

The notice may not be mailed until the due date has passed without payment.

The days of notice are settings, kept in a table by state that the business can maintain. They are
not built into the calculation. See `../sources.md` for where each number comes from and what still
needs checking.

## Calculation examples

| ID  | State | Notice mailed | → Days of notice | → Earliest cancellation date | Rationale                               |
| --- | ----- | ------------- | ---------------- | ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| E1  | FL    | 2027-02-10    | 10               | 2027-02-20                   |                                         |
| E2  | CA    | 2027-02-10    | 10               | 2027-02-20                   |                                         |
| E3  | NY    | 2027-02-10    | 15               | 2027-02-25                   | New York gives five more days           |
| E4  | TX    | 2027-02-10    | 10               | 2027-02-20                   |                                         |
| E5  | NY    | 2027-02-14    | 15               | 2027-03-01                   | The count runs past the end of February |
