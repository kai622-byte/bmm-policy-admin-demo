# R-010 · A supervisor approves or declines a change sent for approval

## Rule

A change is sent for approval when any approval rule applies (R-007, R-009). Every reason that
applies is shown, in the order of the rules, separated by "; ". The representative cannot issue the
change until a supervisor approves it. A supervisor cannot approve a change they entered themselves.

When the supervisor approves, the change is issued (R-006). When the supervisor declines, they must
give a reason, nothing on the policy changes, and the representative sees the reason.
